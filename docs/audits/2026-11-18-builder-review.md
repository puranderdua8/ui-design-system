## Leads (not confirmed, no finding yet)

- **Storage size.** Each project keeps up to 100 full states; several projects could reach the localStorage limit sooner than the "storage full" message suggests. Not measured.
- **Scrollable preview.** At narrow widths the pinned preview has `max-h-[40dvh] overflow-y-auto`. axe is clean, but a keyboard-only pass of that region at 200% zoom in landscape was not done.
- **Screen readers.** The spec's repeated screen-reader script (4.11) was not run in this review.

## What held up

- Guided copy: the banned-word list appears only in the test list in `content/builder.ts`, not in Guided strings; technical names (`--ds-*`, WCAG) appear only in Use it, Check details and the style guide.
- D-144: `stripHash` removes the URL hash before analytics; the share decoder caps encoded and decoded size; the project file reader caps size and goes through the grammar; the only `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` is the theme init script in `app/layout.tsx:77`; preview links and forms are blocked (`playground/preview.tsx:51-53`).
- Each step has its own title and `aria-current="step"`, focus moves to the step heading after the first load, and all Builder pages pass axe with no sideways scroll at 320px and at 200% zoom in three browsers (e2e above).
